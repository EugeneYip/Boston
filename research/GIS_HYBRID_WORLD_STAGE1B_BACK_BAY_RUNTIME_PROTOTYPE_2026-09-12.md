# Stage 1B — Back Bay X/Z-only isolated runtime prototype

**Date** 2026-09-12 · **Baseline** `eea6333a6e15f6a0d6817f4de8c23f9c7190e294` (HEAD == origin/main, clean, 0/0)
**Scope** Back Bay only · X/Z only · default OFF · reversible · local, unpushed.
**No** citywide migration, North End runtime, road/sidewalk/terrain/collision migration, vertical GIS
integration, MapLibre, second renderer, new dependency, or deployment.

---

## A. Executive verdict

# PARTIAL

**The machinery works. The value was not demonstrated, and the implementation has a real defect.**

What was proven:

- **Factual irregular footprints render through Boston's existing procedural visual system.** Rings of 6–39
  vertices went through the same clip, facade grammar, district style, storey generation, chunking and LOD as
  every other building, with **no second renderer, no new material, and no facade engine**. Draw calls moved
  by ≤3 across paired views.
- **Default-off is exact.** With the flag absent, `gisLedger` is `null`, city plots are 10,951 and Back Bay
  buildings 361 — identical to baseline.
- **Ownership is unambiguous.** Baseline Back Bay contains **361 buildings, every one a 4-vertex quad**.
  With the flag on: **12 non-quad buildings**, at exactly the replacement sites, **zero duplicates**.
- **Height stayed procedural.** Replaced buildings generate 13.5–25.1 m over 4–7 storeys from
  `Facades.makeSpec`, exactly as baseline. No GIS elevation, height or datum was consumed.

Why not PASS:

- **Two of fourteen accepted replacements render nothing** — they suppress parcels and leave a hole.
  Arithmetic: 361 − 23 suppressed + 12 rendered = 350 observed, against 14 accepted.
- **Only 12 of 110 candidates (10.9%) reach the screen**, and only 14 of 110 even find a procedural parcel to
  replace. **77 of 110 factual footprints have no procedural counterpart at all.**
- **No view is materially better.** With 12 of 361 buildings changed (3.3%), the paired captures are close to
  indistinguishable at street level. Geographic accuracy improved; the scene did not visibly improve, and the
  brief is explicit that accuracy alone is not sufficient for PASS.

## G. Runtime insertion seam

**One field, one call site.** `RoadNetwork.buildPlots` publishes parcels; `Buildings._collectPlots` consumes
them. The prototype swaps **`plot.polygon`** for a factual ring on a small subset and changes nothing else —
clip, `_respec`, `makeSpec`, district style, storeys, chunking, LOD and colliders all run unmodified.

Production diff: **`src/world/Buildings.js`, +29 lines, guarded**; plus two new files. No parallel city
renderer, no GIS tag parsing in runtime, no new dependency.

## I. Association / suppression method

A candidate claims a parcel when the parcel's centroid is inside the footprint, or ≥3 of its corners are.
Gates, all conservative, any doubt → procedural: no claim · parcel claimed by two candidates · candidate
centroid `isReserved` · a suppressed parcel not covered by the candidate · an unsuppressed parcel overlapping
the candidate · candidate would not survive the building pipeline.

**A symmetric claim test was tried and measured worse** — 12 replacements against 14, because it put 22
parcels into contention — and was reverted. Claiming more was not the goal.

## K. Geometry / facade compatibility

**This is the genuinely positive result.** Ring vertices 7–80 (median 12); **zero were quads**. All went
through `_clipParcel` and `makeSpec` without special handling. No triangulation failure, no facade-generation
failure, no NaN, no z-fighting observed. Corridor clipping rose 15 → 24, which is the factual footprints
meeting Boston's roads — expected, and handled by existing machinery.

## L. Debug ownership result

Performed **structurally rather than chromatically**, and it is stronger for it: baseline Back Bay contains
**361 buildings and every single one is a 4-vertex quad**, because every baseline building is a road-derived
parcel. Any non-quad in the box is therefore necessarily factual. Candidate: **12 non-quad**, at the
replacement coordinates, each at a distinct location — **0 duplicates, 0 doubled masses**.

Two false starts are recorded because they nearly produced wrong conclusions: a centroid-in-ring matcher
reported a hole that was a clipped building 11 m away, and an exact-vertex-signature matcher reported zero
ownership because specs are re-oriented downstream. The vertex-count argument needs neither.

## Q. Fallback behaviour

96 of 110 candidates fell back and rendered as baseline Boston. All five fallback states behaved as designed;
**no factual candidate was forced to raise coverage**, including the two occasions where doing so was
tempting.

## R. Known limitations

1. **2 holes** — see §A and the defect note in `GisBackBay.js`. `survives()` replays only the clip and
   `makeSpec` minimums, not `_respec` / `orientOutward` / `_fitOrnament`. **Fix: emit the spec first and accept
   the suppression only if one materialised** — invert the order rather than predict it.
2. **10.9% of candidates reach the screen.** The bottleneck is not the gate — it is that **77 of 110 factual
   footprints have no procedural parcel to replace**, because Boston subdivides Back Bay into 389 road-derived
   lots where the factual layer has 110 buildings, offset by the very displacement Stage 1A measured.
3. **Roof-break outlines are not ground footprints.** Usable for position and plan shape, not for a
   ground-floor wall line.
4. Two views unpaired (B, D).
5. Debug ownership was structural, not chromatic.

## S. Road / terrain / collision exclusions

**Nothing was migrated.** Roads, sidewalks, kerbs, terrain, water, parks, traffic, pedestrian routing and
landmarks are untouched. **Collision was not migrated**, so a replaced building's colliders still follow the
baseline parcel and can differ from the visible factual mass. All judgement used fixed cameras; no free-roam
validation was attempted and no collision mismatch was read as a visual failure.

Where factual footprints met Boston's roads, the existing corridor clip handled it (clipped 15 → 24). That is
**building improvement exposing road error**, recorded and deliberately not acted on.

## T. Verdict — **PARTIAL** · U. Owner visual acceptance — **OUTSTANDING**

The mechanism is proven and the art direction survives; the value is not yet demonstrated and two holes must
be fixed. This is not a citywide result: North End was not rendered, and Stage 1A.2's identity and
delineation findings stand unchanged.

## V. Next decision required

Whether to authorise a **bounded Stage 1B.1** that (a) inverts the suppression order to eliminate the holes,
and (b) raises association coverage — most plausibly by allowing one factual footprint to replace a *run* of
adjacent parcels, which is what the 77 unmatched candidates need. Without (b) the experiment cannot show
visual value, because 3.3% of a block is not visible. **Nothing here authorises citywide migration, North End
work, or deployment.**
